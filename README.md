# 🐄 DairyTrack — My Smart Dairy Farm

DairyTrack is a smart, offline-first dairy farm management application designed to simplify everyday dairy farming operations.

Built with native Android technologies, DairyTrack helps farmers manage animals, monitor milk production, track breeding and calving activities, maintain health records, and manage farm finances from their smartphones.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Animal Management:** Maintain animal profiles, identification and lifecycle records.
- **Milk Collection:** Record combined morning and evening milk production.
- **Breeding Management:** Track heat detection, semen insertion and pregnancy.
- **Calving & Calf Management:** Maintain delivery records and calf details.
- **Health Management:** Track illnesses, medicines, veterinary visits and vaccinations.
- **Feed Management:** Monitor feed consumption, inventory and expenses.
- **Financial Management:** Manage farm income, expenses, sales and profitability.
- **Worker Management:** Provide restricted data-entry access for farm workers.
- **Multi-User Synchronization:** Share farm data between the administrator, farm member and worker.
- **Offline-First:** Continue recording farm operations without an internet connection.
- **Reports & Analytics:** Monitor farm productivity and financial performance.
- **Backup & Restore:** Protect important farm records.

## 👥 User Roles

1. **Administrator:** Complete farm management and user administration.
2. **Farm Member:** Full farm and financial management access.
3. **Farm Worker:** Restricted access to combined milk collection, semen insertion and calving entries.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- Kotlin
- Jetpack Compose
- Material 3
- Room / SQLite
- Firebase Authentication
- Cloud Firestore
- WorkManager

## 🎯 Project Vision

To make dairy farm management simpler, more organized and accessible through technology, while helping farmers maintain accurate records and make better operational decisions.
